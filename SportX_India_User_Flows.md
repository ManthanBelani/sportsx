# SportX India — User Flows

This document outlines the core user journeys for each of the four main roles in the SportX India MVP application, based on the feature specifications.

---

## 1. Athlete User Flow
Athletes use the platform to build their digital identity, network with peers, and find opportunities.

1. **Onboarding & Registration:** The athlete registers on the Android app and logs in.
2. **Profile Creation:** 
   - Uploads profile photo, enters bio, primary sport, and location.
   - Adds achievements, certificates, performance stats, and highlight media (photos/videos).
3. **Networking (Athlete-to-Athlete):**
   - Browses the Athlete Directory using filters (sport, location, age, level).
   - Sends connection requests to fellow athletes.
   - Uses basic in-app messaging to communicate with connected peers.
4. **Exploring Academies/Coaches:**
   - Navigates to the Academies & Coaches directory.
   - Searches for local training programs, views credentials, and finds trials.
5. **Seeking Sponsorships:**
   - Navigates to the Sponsors & Opportunities section.
   - Browses active sponsorship listings and expresses interest/applies.
6. **Engagement:** Receives push notifications tailored to their sport and region regarding trials, selections, and new opportunities.

---

## 2. Coach / Academy User Flow
Coaches and Academies use the platform to establish credibility, recruit athletes, and find sponsorships.

1. **Onboarding & Registration:** The coach or academy representative registers and logs in.
2. **Profile Setup:**
   - Uploads academy logo or professional photo.
   - Details sport specialisations, location, credentials, facilities, and programs offered.
3. **Talent Discovery:**
   - Accesses the Athletes Directory.
   - Filters athletes by sport, location, and achievement level to identify potential recruits or trial candidates.
4. **Seeking Support:**
   - Browses the Sponsors section for potential institutional partnerships, equipment support, or event funding.
5. **Engagement:** Submits opportunities (like camps or trials) for Admin approval, and receives notifications relevant to their sport.

---

## 3. Sponsor User Flow
Sponsors use the platform to discover talent and build partnerships within the sports community.

1. **Onboarding & Registration:** The sponsor brand/organisation registers and logs in.
2. **Brand Profile Creation:**
   - Sets up a profile with a logo, organisation description, industry category, and coverage area.
   - Lists active opportunities (sponsorships, funding).
3. **Athlete Scouting:**
   - Browses the Athletes Directory.
   - Shortlists athletes who align with their brand focus based on performance data and highlight media.
4. **Academy Partnerships:**
   - Browses the Coaches & Academies Directory.
   - Identifies academies for potential institutional partnerships or event sponsorships.

---

## 4. Admin User Flow (Web Panel)
The Admin manages the platform's integrity, users, and content from a web-based dashboard.

1. **Login:** Accesses the separate web-based Admin Panel.
2. **User & Profile Management:**
   - Reviews new user registrations.
   - Verifies accounts and assigns badges (e.g., verified athletes or academies).
   - Suspends or removes users violating platform rules.
3. **Content Moderation:**
   - Reviews and approves/rejects opportunity listings submitted by Sponsors or Academies before they become visible to Athletes.
   - Monitors platform-wide posts and removes inappropriate content.
4. **Notification Broadcasting:**
   - Composes push notifications.
   - Targets broadcasts by specific roles (e.g., Athletes only), sport category, or geographic region.
5. **Monitoring Health:** Views dashboard metrics for user counts by role, region, and activity.
