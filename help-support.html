Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a trial?
Browse trials in the Trials section, select one that interests you, and tap "Register for Trial". Fill in your details, upload required documents, and pay the entry fee to confirm your registration.
How do I save items for later?
Tap the heart icon on any academy, coach, trial, or tournament to save it to your Saved Items. Access all your saved content from the Saved tab in the bottom navigation.
Can I edit my profile after signing up?
Yes! Go to your Profile and tap "Edit Profile" to update your name, sport preferences, skill level, achievements, and profile photo.
How do I report a listing?
If you find inappropriate or incorrect content, tap the "Report" link on the listing detail page. Our team reviews all reports within 24-48 hours.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All payments are secured with industry-standard encryption.
