Common questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for coaches and academies with up to 25 students.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately.

What happens if I exceed my student limit?

You'll receive a notification. Upgrade or continue with overage charges.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, UPI, net banking, and Rupay via Razorpay.
