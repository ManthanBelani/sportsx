Changelog

What's new in SportX — updates, improvements, and fixes.

Back to About
Version 1.4.0
New Release
January 15, 2026

New Features

Tournament brackets — Auto-generated brackets for tournaments with up to 64 teams, with live score updates
Sponsorship dashboard — Sponsors can now create, manage, and track their sponsorship programs
Scholarship finder — New filterable feed for sports scholarships with eligibility criteria and direct apply
Multi-language support — App now available in Hindi and Tamil, with more languages coming

Improvements

Search relevance — Improved academy and coach search with sport-first ranking
Registration flow — Reduced trial registration from 5 steps to 2
Profile completeness — New progress bar, boosting visibility in sponsor discovery

Bug Fixes

Fixed crash on Android 14 when opening notification center
Resolved issue where saved items weren't syncing across devices
Fixed incorrect seat availability display on high-demand trials
Version 1.3.0
New Release
December 1, 2025

New Features

Coach enquiry inbox — Dedicated inbox for coaches to manage and respond to athlete enquiries
Academy trial posting — Academies can post trials with eligibility criteria and seat limits
Media gallery — Athletes can upload photos and videos to their profiles
Universal search — Search across academies, coaches, trials, tournaments, and scholarships

Improvements

Onboarding — Streamlined 3-step onboarding with sport selection and role picker
Performance — App launch time reduced by 40% on mid-range devices

Bug Fixes

Fixed OTP not arriving for some Airtel numbers
Resolved filter panel not resetting on back navigation
Version 1.0.0
Launch
July 1, 2025

New Features

SportX launches — Initial release with academy & coach directory, trial listings, and tournament calendar
Role-based dashboards — Separate experiences for athletes, coaches, academies, organizers, sponsors, and admins
Enquiry system — Direct enquiries to coaches and academies from their profiles
UPI payments — Full UPI integration for tournament registrations
