At SportX Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("SportX", "we", "our", or "us"), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use certain features, you may provide us with:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, date of birth, profile photo, and password
  • Role-specific information — for athletes: sport preferences, achievements, media galleries; for coaches: credentials, certifications, coaching history
  • Content you submit — enquiries, applications, reviews, messages, and other communications
  • Verification documents — government-issued IDs, proof of credentials, business registration documents

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access the Platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device information — device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data — pages viewed, features used, time spent, search queries, clicks
  • Location data — general city/location for personalised recommendations (with your permission)
  • Log data — IP address, browser type, access times, referring URLs

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party sign-in providers (Google, Apple), business partners, and publicly available sources.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and provide access to Platform features
  • Personalise your experience and deliver relevant recommendations
  • Facilitate enquiries, applications, and registrations between users
  • Verify the identity and credentials of coaches, academies, and sponsors
  • Send you notifications about registrations, status updates, reminders, and opportunities
  • Process payments and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Improve our Platform, develop new features, and conduct research
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

3.1 Within the Platform

When you send an enquiry to a coach or apply to an academy, your profile information is shared with that coach or academy. When you apply for a sponsorship, your profile is shared with the sponsoring brand.

3.2 Service Providers

We share information with vendors who help us operate the Platform, including cloud hosting, push notification services, analytics providers, and payment processors (Razorpay, UPI).

3.3 Legal & Safety

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if disclosure is necessary to prevent fraud, protect safety, or enforce our Terms.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and employee security training
  • Secure, ISO 27001 certified cloud infrastructure

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, go to Settings → Privacy → Manage Your Data in the app, or email privacy@sportx.in. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, account data is deleted within 30 days; backup copies may persist for up to 90 days.

7. Children's Privacy

SportX is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For users aged 13–17, we collect limited data with verified parental consent where required.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the Platform and the updated policy will reflect the new "Last updated" date.
