About SportX

Our story in brief.

Our mission

Democratizing access to sport in India.

SportX is India's first unified sports network — a mobile-first platform connecting athletes, coaches, academies, tournament organizers, sponsors, and parents in one place.

Founded: 2024, Mumbai
Team: 25 people across engineering, product, design, and growth
Funding: $5M Series A
Users: Growing across 50+ cities in India
