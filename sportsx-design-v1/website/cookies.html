Cookie Policy

How SportX uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

Back to About

This Cookie Policy explains how SportX Technologies Pvt. Ltd. uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you access our mobile application, website, and related services.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website or app. They help remember your preferences, login state, and how you use the service. Similar technologies include local storage, pixel tags, and web beacons.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your preferences (language, location, sport interests)
  • Understand how you navigate and use the Platform
  • Deliver personalised content and recommendations
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing
  • Prevent fraud and keep the Platform secure

Types of cookies we use

CategoryPurposeDuration
EssentialRequired for core Platform functionality — login, security, session managementSession / Persistent
PreferencesRemember your settings and choices (language, sport preferences, filters)Persistent (up to 1 year)

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics — for usage analytics and behaviour insights
  • Mixpanel / Amplitude — for product analytics and funnel tracking
  • Firebase — for push notifications and crash reporting
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — for authentication
  • Razorpay / UPI — for payment processing security

Managing cookies

You can control or disable cookies through:

  • App settings — Go to Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences
  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies
  • Device settings — iOS and Android allow you to restrict ad tracking

Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect some Platform features.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Any material changes will be communicated via the Platform.
