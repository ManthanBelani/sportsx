What is SportX

India's sports, unified.

India has incredible sporting talent and passion, but the ecosystem is fragmented. Finding the right coach, joining a trial, or landing a sponsorship should not depend on your network or location. SportX brings everyone together in one place.

Discover

Academies, coaches, trials, tournaments, scholarships and sponsors — filtered by sport, location, age and budget.

Connect

One-tap enquiries, registrations, and applications. Real-time status updates so you never miss an opportunity.

Grow

Track your progress, receive recommendations, apply for sponsorships, and take your sport further — from playground to podium.

Six roles, one platform

Everyone in Indian sport belongs here.

SportX isn't just for athletes. It's a complete network serving everyone who plays, trains, organizes, funds, or supports sport.

Athletes & Parents

Find academies and coaches, apply for trials and tournaments, discover scholarships and sponsorships.

Coaches

List services, manage student enquiries, schedule sessions, and grow your reach beyond your neighbourhood.

Academies

Manage your listing, post trials, track applicants, and run operations from one dashboard.

Organizers

Create tournaments, manage registrations, handle capacity, publish results across India.

Sponsors

Discover talent, create sponsorship programs, and manage applications in one place.

Admins

Moderate listings, verify sponsors, manage users, and track platform analytics.
