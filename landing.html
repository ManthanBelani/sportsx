Features

Everything Indian sport needs, in one app.

Every feature in SportX solves a real friction point in the Indian sports journey — from discovery to sponsorship.

Smart discovery

Personalised recommendations based on sport, age group, location and budget — refreshed as new listings drop.

  • Filter by sport, age, city, fees
  • Saved items & activity history
  • Universal search across roles

Trials & tournaments

Browse upcoming trials, register with one tap, get instant confirmations and reminders.

  • Real-time seat availability
  • Eligibility check on register
  • Calendar sync & push reminders

Coach & academy tools

Operators get full dashboards: enquiry inbox, schedule, registrants, profile completeness and analytics.

  • Enquiry inbox with status
  • Trial posting form & drafts
  • Registrant list & detail

Sponsorships & scholarships

A dedicated feed for financial aid and brand support — apply in minutes, track status in real time.

  • Scholarship feed & detail
  • Sponsor discovery for athletes
  • Brand applications inbox
